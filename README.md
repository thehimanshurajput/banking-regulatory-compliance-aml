# Banking Business Analyst | Regulatory Compliance and AML

Independent portfolio case studies showing business analysis for selected banking compliance controls. The scenario covers customer risk classification, customer due diligence (CDD), enhanced due diligence (EDD), fraud alert workflows, audit logging, compliance reporting, and UAT scenario design.

## Portfolio scope

These materials are independent learning and design exercises based on generalized scenarios. They do not represent banking employment, client delivery, production implementation, or executed UAT. Names and IDs in the examples are synthetic. Rules, thresholds, sample values, and outcomes are illustrative assumptions, not regulatory advice or an institution’s policy. The scenarios should be reviewed by qualified compliance and legal stakeholders before any real-world use.

## Case study: Compliance control enhancements

The work explores requirements for risk-based customer review, EDD queue management, fraud alert investigation, auditability, and management reporting. It includes functional user stories, business rules, acceptance criteria, and illustrative test scenarios.
